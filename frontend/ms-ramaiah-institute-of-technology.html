About Ramaiah Institute of Technology (MSRIT)

Ramaiah Institute of Technology, commonly known as MSRIT or MS Ramaiah Institute of Technology, is an engineering institution located in Mathikere, Bengaluru, Karnataka. Established in 1962, the institute offers undergraduate, postgraduate and research programmes across engineering, architecture, management and related disciplines.

Students exploring direct admission in MS Ramaiah Institute of Technology can review the available admission routes, programme requirements and current-year fee information before applying. The institute's official website publishes admission notifications, eligibility details and fee structure documents for each academic year.

MSRIT has academic departments, laboratories, research facilities and a placement and training department. Students should compare course curriculum, specialisation, eligibility, fees and placement information before selecting a programme.

Direct Admission in MS Ramaiah Institute of Technology

Direct admission in MS Ramaiah Institute of Technology is a commonly used search term among students looking for admission options outside the regular entrance-based counselling route. For the 2026–27 academic year, the institute's official website has published an admission notice stating that applications are open for eligible candidates under Management, NRI, PIO and Foreign National quotas.

Management quota admission is subject to the institute's admission rules, eligibility requirements, seat availability, document verification and the applicable fee structure. It is not an automatic or guaranteed admission route. Students should confirm the process and current requirements directly with the MSRIT Admission Section before making any payment or decision.

Admission Routes

  • KEA / KCET counselling: Candidates may seek admission through the Karnataka Examination Authority process, subject to the applicable eligibility and counselling rules.
  • COMEDK UGET counselling: Eligible candidates may participate in COMEDK counselling for seats offered through this route.
  • Management quota: The institute's 2026–27 notice lists management quota admissions. Applicants should contact the official Admission Section for seat availability, eligibility, fees and the application procedure.
  • NRI / PIO / Foreign National categories: Separate provisions and supporting documents apply. Candidates should check the official admission page for the relevant category requirements.

How to Explore Direct Admission

  • Choose the B.E. programme or specialisation you wish to study.
  • Check the academic eligibility criteria and the current admission notification.
  • Contact the MSRIT Admission Section to confirm whether seats are available under the relevant quota.
  • Ask for the official fee details, payment instructions and list of required documents.
  • Submit the application and documents through the process confirmed by the institute.
  • Complete admission formalities only after receiving confirmation from the institute.

Eligibility for B.E. Admission at MS Ramaiah Institute of Technology

As stated on the institute's admission page, applicants for B.E. programmes should have passed the 10+2 or equivalent examination with Physics and Mathematics as compulsory subjects, along with an eligible optional subject such as Chemistry, Biotechnology, Biology, Computer Science or Electronics.

  • General category: The published eligibility criteria specify a minimum aggregate of 45% in the qualifying examination.
  • Reserved categories: The published criteria specify 40% for SC/ST, Category-1 and OBC category candidates, subject to the applicable rules.
  • Entrance examinations: The institute's admission information lists KEA and COMEDK as entrance examination routes for B.E. admissions.

Eligibility conditions can be updated by the institute or the relevant admission authority. Candidates should verify the criteria for their admission year and category on the official website.

Placements at Ramaiah Institute of Technology

Ramaiah Institute of Technology has a Department of Placement and Training that coordinates recruitment activities for students. The department describes activities such as recruitment drives, aptitude preparation, group discussions, personality development and interview preparation.

Placement outcomes can vary by department, graduating batch, student profile, market conditions and recruiter requirements. Students should review the institute's latest placement information and department-wise reports rather than relying on a single package figure.
